Subject: Originality check of a finite-FIFO admission-partition theorem

The model is a known finite arrival trace, an initially empty single FIFO

time varying over a continuous interval. Jobs have a common relative
completion deadline; late jobs remain until completion. The objective is

exact analysis by splitting only on admission, without branching on idle or
deadline events. The packet gives the capacity and deadline thresholds, a
