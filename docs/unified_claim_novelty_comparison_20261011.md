## Why the surviving claim is narrow

The strongest conceptual neighbors cover robustness, uncertainty-aware action
and separate forecasting/classification evaluation. Galileo studies environmental
perturbations and controller effects; SuanMing reports error and confusion
metrics together; Sinan uses different prediction objectives. Our evidence
adds specific measured behavior for the inexpensive procedures and ordinary
Linux contracts in this artifact. Exact benchmark mismatch does not show that
the insight is absent from their work or the wider literature.

The quantitative contribution therefore rests on the connected empirical
record: an ordinary nonlinear control narrows the capacity result; separate
deadline tests expose different adequacy; matching controls preserve both gains
and failures; fresh decision tests retain one narrow event and a negative
non-reproduction; remedy benefits are accompanied by reduced coverage and
measured acquisition costs. These observations justify a scoped case-study
manuscript. They do not establish broad scientific originality or acceptance.

## What was actually compared

Executed methods are the artifact's whole-run, idle-corrected, busy, linear
and quadratic capacity inputs; simplified constant/periodic service; queue-adjusted
and complete-response CDFs; pooled/policy/rate matching; recent-25 and weighted
updates; and registered ordinary sampling bands. None is a faithful implementation
of a complete published predictor in the table. Published controls often use
richer state, training, perturbations or online feedback. Their inputs and costs
cannot be discarded and their names attached to a simple local baseline.

No published-system head-to-head result is claimed. A large V30 gain partly
reflects the fluid comparator's deterministic 28.640 ms floor above a 25 ms
deadline. Policy pooling is intentionally mismatched. Rate matching yields
no required extra gain over the ordinary reference. The ordinary V31 complete-
response control weakens decomposition novelty. Those comparisons remain
central rather than being relegated to favorable interpretation.
