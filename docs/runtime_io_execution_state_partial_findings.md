# Execution-state pilot: useful partial evidence, mechanism unresolved

Reviewed 2026-10-07. No new benchmark or host-setting change. Retain the
interrupted generation-02 and its original failed-attempt markers. This is a
partial analysis, not the protocol's final sixteen-cell decision.

The [partial report](../analysis/runtime_io_execution_state_pilot/generation-02/partial_analysis/1791313749375911467/report.md)
was generated by [the raw reviewer](../analysis/review_execution_state_partial.py).
All twelve completed cells independently replay identically to their saved
results. Hardware fixtures, intervention receipts and restored original CPU
bounds verify. The thirteenth cell, b2_D, failed during settling before creating
its workload container/cgroup; b2_C/B/A were unrun.

## What worked

The entire first block passed the practical observer and execution-separation
gate. Compared with automatic mode, the constrained bounds reduced the effective
cycles/user CPU-second proxy by 23.63% at 100 ms and 24.14% at 20 ms. Whole-job CPU
cost rose roughly 30–31% and capacity fell roughly 23–24%, with average CPU supply
remaining close to 0.5 core. Instructions/job remained around 26.709 million,
with about 0.002% span across the six counted cells. These observations establish
that the pilot can produce distinguishable measured execution conditions for
this workload; reduced clock requests are not verified constant physical clocks.

All four first-block and both available second-block observer comparisons pass
the predeclared 1% CPU/job, capacity and delivery margins. This is a practical
screen, not statistical equivalence or proof that common helper/host observation
has zero overhead.

Counted period contrasts are also small improvements: -0.675% automatic block1,
-0.518% constrained block1, and -1.545% constrained block2. The original large
shorter-period CPU penalty was absent in every available paired comparison.

First-block interaction is -0.816% without counters and +0.158% with counters.
Neither reaches the predeclared 2% practical signal; their directions differ.
Slowing both periods substantially therefore did not produce a clear period
interaction in the complete block. The automatic-speed second-block cells are
missing, so there is no complete reverse-block interaction, replication decision,
or basis to declare all execution-state explanations excluded.

## Why settling stopped

The final minute was already steady: mean 56.933 C, range 0.125 C and slope
-0.0987 C/min. The automatic-mode baseline was 62.721 C, so the final mean was
5.788 C cooler, outside the required +/-3 C band. The accepted automatic band
was approximately 59.72–65.72 C. The 10-minute limit was reached while that
comparability criterion failed. This was not proof of overheating or ongoing
large temperature fluctuations. More waiting alone need not return a cooler
stable machine to its earlier baseline.

The recorded policy remained comparable during this settling attempt. The
cause of the lower steady temperature is not established.

## Additional limits

The host sampler exited -9 during cleanup and its 412 MB JSON is truncated.
A bounded streaming read recovered 4,931 complete prefix records ending only
about five seconds into the failed settling. It cannot establish background
activity during the later cooling. Trial-local sealed speed/power samples still
cover all completed measurement windows and pass replay; the host log failure
must nevertheless be reported, not converted into a successful whole run.

One counted constrained 20 ms reverse cell, b2_F, attributes 5.463% of its CPU
time to system CPU, versus roughly 0.23–0.46% in the other counted cells. This
makes its +7.188% effective user cycle-rate contrast sensitive to the user-CPU
denominator. Native and cgroup total CPU agree, and user instruction/CPI changes
remain tiny. There is no evidence here that uniquely identifies actual clocks,
kernel work or accounting behavior as the reason for that split. Do not call
this a fixed-clock result or an explanation of the original mostly-user-CPU
penalty.

## Decision

This is informative partial evidence: feasibility succeeded in the complete
block, but the tested execution-control intervention has not supplied a
promising large period interaction or reproduced the old penalty. Recommend
retaining this result without another run solely to reach sixteen cells.

The missing cells are necessary for the protocol's planned two-block conclusion,
not for recognizing its partial findings. If a formal replicated causal claim
becomes necessary, address the drifting thermal reference and host-log
finalization in a new predeclared balanced protocol. Do not merge cells from a
later session into this interrupted run or silently relax its rules. No such
new run is prepared or launched here. Historical cause and novelty remain
unestablished; the earlier queue/yielding findings remain usable with their
existing limits.
