# Pre-validation timing-shift signal: bounded development protocol

10 October 2026. This is a new saved-data assessment requested after manuscript integration. All historical outcomes are already known. The numerical candidate score has not yet been computed. Original experiments and manuscript gates remain unchanged.

The candidate is a **deadline-amplified timing shift**, accompanied by a chronological warmup check. Estimate delta = median(W) - median(C) from the last 25 completed warmup responses W and the permissible complete-response calibration C. Predict p_shift = 1 - F_C(D-delta). The warning score is the maximum of |p_shift-p_reference| and the difference between translated predictions using the first and last 12 warmup offers. The center offer is excluded only from the two-half comparison. Refuse an assurance when the score exceeds the existing 5 percentage point tolerance or a calibration/warmup offer fails.

Under an actual persistent location shift, the CDF displacement gives the probability change exactly at population level. Real response shapes, reference estimation error, warmup sampling noise and subsequent changes can invalidate that premise. This is a candidate input-to-future relation, not a bound on future state. Median translation is ordinary distribution modelling; any contribution must come from evidence of useful advance reliability information rather than naming it a new estimator. The temporal check tests whether timing is still moving within warmup; it cannot see a jump after warmup.

All four completed campaigns are development evidence: 18 Go V31, 18 original NGINX, 24 policy confirmation and 12 rate-transfer cohorts. Preserve the frozen primary deadlines. Use the complete-response Go reference, original NGINX reference, matched-policy power reference and ordinary fixed-5/s rate reference. The rate-matched pool will not silently provide extra acquisition budget.

Compare ordinary recent empirical CDF, sample-weighted update, the identical median-translation predictor, direct deadline disagreement, chronological Bernoulli disagreement and full ECDF sup distance. Give every sample-based method the same 25 offers and decision time. Charge the full acquired reference and full apparatus warmup separately; using only a suffix does not prove deployment cost savings. The 25 offers take about 6 seconds at 4/s or 3 seconds at 8/s before drain and control overhead. Timing checks that precede these offers retain their earlier-information status.

Report answer coverage, accepted reference MAE and maximum error, accepted errors over 5 pp, caught failures, false warnings and per-campaign transition-region coverage. Keep each campaign separate; retain saturated P1 cases and show their contribution explicitly. Report median-shift corrected forecast accuracy alongside direct warmup profiling; report shift-only and temporal-only ablations without choosing the best afterward. Quantile displacement and centered ECDF distance are descriptive shape checks with no fitted gate. No p-value or future-risk guarantee follows from a small empirical distance.

Freeze the machine-readable design and this note before computing scores. Reconstruct values from sealed raw records and reconcile original forecasts/outcomes. Do not tune thresholds, windows, endpoints or blends. Do not launch traffic. A survivor needs independent calibration-to-future episodes and useful performance against these same-information ordinary comparators. A failure is retained as a bounded feasibility result.
